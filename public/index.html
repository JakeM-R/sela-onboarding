From application to day one

Paste a job description and one candidate's CV and answers. It builds a rubric on Sela's three hiring values plus the role's must-haves, scores the candidate with the application line behind every score, writes the interview questions, drafts the reply, and on hire writes the onboarding pack.

Built by Jake Makking-Robinson for Sela's strategy & ops role. A working draft, not a Sela product.
Under a second per candidateagainst an estimated 20–30 minutes to read, score and reply by hand. It drafts; a person makes every decision. Nothing you paste leaves this page.

        How it scores. The three hiring-value axes and their 1/3/5 anchors are fixed. Must-haves are the requirement lines in the job description, minus nice-to-haves and anything the values already cover. A score rises with lines that show an action, a number and an outcome, and falls with buzzwords. Every quote is copied from the pasted text, never written. The suggestion is a fixed rule: any must-have at 2 or below suggests decline.
        
    
        What this version is. A transparent rules engine that runs in your browser: no model, no server, no stored data. Both postings are Sela's own. Every demo candidate is synthetic except one real profile, the applicant's, which is scored by the same rules as everyone else. A production version would swap the matching step for a language model and keep the same rubric, quoting and decision rule.
