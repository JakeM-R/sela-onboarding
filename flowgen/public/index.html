        The rules
        
    Dates come from the start date, not a spreadsheet
    
        
      
          
    • Every task has an owner and a day offset. “Laptop ordered: day −14, Operations” is due 14 days before the start date. A due date that lands on a weekend moves to the Friday before.
      • 
          
    • Tasks fall into Before day 1 (offset below 0), Week 1 (0 to 6), Weeks 2–4 (7 to 27) and Day 30 (28+).
      • 
          
    • Overdue: the due date has passed and it isn't ticked. At risk: due within 2 days and not ticked.
      • 
          
    • The blocked strip ranks owners by how many hires their overdue tasks hold up, then by task count, then by how late.
      • 
          
    • A role gets the common tasks, its family's tasks (deployment or sales), then its own. “manager” resolves to the manager picked for that hire.
