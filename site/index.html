Method

Cost per physical GPU-hour

Buy: (GPU + server share) ÷ (life × 8,760 h), plus colocation per kW ÷ 730 h, plus metered power. Power scales between idle and peak draw with utilisation, times PUE. Lease: monthly rate ÷ 730 h, with hosting and power included. Rent-in: the hourly rate on every held hour, minus the idle hours you release.

Revenue and margin

Revenue per physical GPU-hour = realised price × utilisation × pooling factor. Margin = revenue − cost. Break-even is the utilisation at which margin is zero. Crossovers are the utilisations at which two options earn the same.

Simulation

5,000 draws, each sampling utilisation and realised price uniformly from your ranges and a rent-in price from its range. Cards show the median and the 10th–90th percentile band, and "ahead in" is the share of draws where an option earns the most. The seed is fixed, so the same inputs always give the same output.

Out of scope: financing and cost of capital, tax, residual resale value, staff and software overhead, multi-GPU cluster networking, and per-second sandbox pricing. A real decision would also price lease and rent-in availability risk, which this model doesn't.
